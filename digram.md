# Air Force Flight Training Management System

Kuwait Government Milatary Traning Force

## Goal

- To maintain a centralized, real-time picture of every pilot’s training status and operational readiness, and help Air Force commanders/instructors decide whether a pilot is ready and authorized to perform missions.

## Problem it tracks

- How do we know, at any moment, which pilots are actually ready to fly operational missions, why they are/aren't ready, and what needs to be done to make them ready?
