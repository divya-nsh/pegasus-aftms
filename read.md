# Air Force Flight Training Management System

Client is kuwait goverment

- This system manages military flight training, pilot readiness, missions, evaluations,
  qualifications, medical fitness, KPI scoring, reports and notifications.

2. Major Modules
    Dashboard
    Pilot Management
    Flight Log
    Mission Planning & Scheduling
    Mission Evaluation
    KPI Dashboard
    Qualifications & Currency
    Medical
    Physical Fitness
    Training
    Reports
    Notifications
    Administration & Roles
3. Workflow
4. Administrator creates users, roles, aircraft and master data.
5. Pilot profile is created.
6. Instructor schedules training mission.
7. Trainee receives assignment.
8. Mission executed and flight log completed.
9. Instructor performs evaluation using KPI categories.
10. System calculates scores/readiness automatically.
11. Reports and dashboards updated; notifications generated for expiries.
12. Pilot Profile
     Personal Information
     Rank
     Squadron
     Aircraft Type
     Instructor Status
     Mission Commander Qualification
     Medical Status
     Security Clearance
     Total Flight Hours
13. Mission Evaluation
    Mission Information: Mission No., Aircraft, Date, Instructor, Weather, Mission Type.
    Evaluation categories: Flight Proficiency (25%), Tactical Skills (25%), Mission Performance
    (20%), Safety (15%), Leadership (10%), Physical Readiness (5%).
14. Readiness Dashboard
     Combat Ready Pilots
     Non Combat Ready
     Medical Expired
     Qualification Expiring
     Low Flight Hours
     Overdue Evaluations
     Safety Violations
     Instructor Availability
15. Reports
     Trainee Progress Report
     Instructor Performance
     Mission History
     Flight Hours
     Qualification Expiry
     Medical Status
     KPI Summary
     Aircraft Utilization
     Attendance
     Audit Log
16. Functional Requirements
    Role-based access, document attachments, search/filter, approval workflow, email/SMS
    notifications, audit trail, dashboard charts, export to PDF/Excel, secure authentication.
17. Non-Functional Requirements
    Responsive web application, SQL database, API integration, encrypted communication,
    backup & recovery, activity logging, high availability.
